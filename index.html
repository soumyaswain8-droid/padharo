Children's play areaAmenity guide

A safe, fenced play area for the society's children — open through the day and maintained by society staff.

🕒 Timings

Daily 6:00 AM–8:00 PM. Lights till 8:30 PM.

🛠️ Upkeep

Equipment checked weekly by staff. Report any damage under Complaints in the app.

Do's

  • Children under 8 with an adult
  • Take turns on the equipment
  • Keep the area clean

Don'ts

  • No cycling or skating inside
  • No food near the equipment
  • No pets in the play area
  • No use after dark

Community hallAmenity guide

The multipurpose hall is for birthdays, pujas, society meetings and small functions. Reserve it through the app — the committee approves within 24 hours.

🕒 Timings

8:00 AM–10:00 PM daily. Quiet hours after 10 PM.

🎟️ How to book

Padharo → Amenities → Community hall → choose date & time. ₹500 refundable deposit.

Do's

  • Clean up after your event
  • Inform the committee of guest count
  • Return furniture as arranged

Don'ts

  • No loud music after 10 PM
  • No commercial / paid use
  • No nailing or sticking on walls
  • No overnight stay
Reserve in the app →
Gardens & lawnAmenity guide

Shared green space for walks, play and morning yoga. Maintained daily by society staff — their check-in is tracked in the app.

🕒 Open hours

5:00 AM–10:00 PM. Lights off at 10:30 PM.

🧹 Upkeep

Gardener checks in via the guard app each morning. Report issues under Complaints.

Do's

  • Keep it clean — use the dustbins
  • Pets on a leash
  • Supervise children at the play area

Don'ts

  • No plucking flowers or plants
  • No cycling on the lawn
  • No parking
  • No littering

Main gate & securityAmenity guide

Every visitor is logged and approved before entry. The guard uses Padharo to notify your flat in seconds — no more strangers walking in.

🕒 Manning

Guarded 24×7. Vehicle gate auto-closes; pedestrian gate via the guard.

🛡️ How entry works

Guest arrives → guard logs them → you approve on your phone. Pre-approve deliveries & cabs for a gate code.

Do's

  • Pre-approve expected guests & deliveries
  • Keep your flat profile updated
  • Collect parcels promptly

Don'ts

  • Don't share gate codes publicly
  • Don't let tailgaters in behind you
  • Don't send staff in without registering them

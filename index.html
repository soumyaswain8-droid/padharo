Five things, done well

Everything a small society needs.

Not 250 features you'll never open — the handful your committee actually uses, every single day.

🛡️

Visitor approval

The guard logs a visitor in three taps; the flat approves on their phone before anyone comes up. Works even after the guard changes — no multi-day training.

📦

Delivery & cabs

Residents pre-approve an Amazon parcel or a cab and get a gate code. The guard just waves them in.

💳

Maintenance

One-tap UPI collection with a live defaulters list. Stop chasing dues on WhatsApp — the treasurer sees who's paid at a glance.

📢

Notices

One clean broadcast to every flat. No ads burying the water-cut notice — finally off the noisy WhatsApp group.

🛠️

Complaints

Residents log it, the committee resolves it, everyone sees status. The 11 PM calls to the secretary stop.

📇

Resident directory

Every flat, every contact, in one place. The whole society, organised — set up in ten minutes.
